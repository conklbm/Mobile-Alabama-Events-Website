    2026 Eagle Award Speaker - Nicole Walters

    Nicole Walters is a New York Times bestselling author\, entrepreneur\, CEO of Inherit Learning Company\, and sought-after keynote speaker who believes we all have the power to create a life that feels meaningful\, joyful\, and truly our own. Her mission is to help people live better\, pursue their purpose\, and build a legacy that lasts.

With faith\, courage\, and a willingness to ask for help\, she turned her side hustle into a full-time business in just six months and went on to build a multimillion-dollar personal development and business education company.

    About the Eagle Award

    The eagle symbolizes strength\, vision and the ability to rise above &mdash\;qualities that have defined generations of minority entrepreneurs who have built successful businesses\, created opportunities and strengthened our community.

    For nearly three decades\, the Mobile Chamber Eagle Awards have honored more than 130 outstanding minority-owned companies for their achievements\, perseverance\, growth and contributions to our economy.

    We want to hear your company'\;s story&mdash\;what you'\;ve built\, what you'\;ve accomplished and the impact you'\;re making.

    Eligibility

    Applicants must:

    • Be a minority-owned business\, with at least 51% ownership\, operation and control by African American\, Hispanic/Latino\, American Indian or Asian American individuals.
    • Must be a Chamber member upon selection.
    • Have been in business for at least three years.
    • Have 100 or fewer employees.
    • Be a privately owned\, for-profit business.
    • Be headquartered in Mobile\, Baldwin or Washington County.
    • Eagle Award winners may reapply after one year.

    The day includes the Eagle Business Forum\, a professional development program\, and culminates with the annual Eagle Awards. \;

    The deadline to Apply for Eagle Award \;is Monday\, September 28.
