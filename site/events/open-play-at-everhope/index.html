About

Come join us at EverHope Baptist Church for a fun filled Story Time for kids! We will meet every Tuesday at 9:30AM. (Located across the street from Baker High School.) Story time will occur 2x a month & the other 2x a month we will have open play with toys & playtime on the playground! We will have stories, music, crafts, and play time! All ages welcome. We will also have snacks for the kids and coffee & treats for parents! We can’t wait to see you! Join their facebook group for more info: https://www.facebook.com/share/g/1Jp9PvkFvv/
