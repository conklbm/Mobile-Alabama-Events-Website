About

Step into a world of creativity and fall fun at Scarecrows in the Gardens—an enchanting fall experience like no other! Stroll through the Gardens and discover a delightful display of scarecrows, each uniquely designed and crafted by local organizations, schools, and businesses. From spooky to silly, these charming characters are scattered throughout the scenic pathways, adding a playful touch to your fall visit. Whether you’re bringing the family, snapping photos, or simply soaking in the seasonal atmosphere, Scarecrows in the Gardens offers something magical for everyone. Don’t forget to vote for your favorite scarecrow as you explore the displays! Join us for this fun-filled celebration of autumn and creativity, and make sure to experience all the festive events happening during The Great Bellingrath Harvest.
