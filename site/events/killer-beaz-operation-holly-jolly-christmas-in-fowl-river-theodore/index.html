    Killer Beaz "Operation: Holly Jolly Christmas" in Fowl River, Theodore

    Sat Dec 12 · 6:30 pm – 8:30 pm

    Dauphin Island · Dauphin Island, AL

    About

    Comedian, television personality, and Grand Ole Opry regular Killer Beaz is bringing a holiday mission to the stage with the Killer Beaz "Operation: Holly Jolly Christmas" Tour, coming to American Legion Post #250 Fowl River in Theodore, AL on Saturday, December 12 at 6:30 PM. The mission: Big Laughs big, belly laughs for the holiday season all while giving back, as a portion of the proceeds will benefit American Legion Post #250 and their Toys For Tots Drive! Featuring the Southern storytelling, quick wit, and hilariously charming personality that have defined his career, the tour brings an evening of festive fun, seasonal cheer, and "killer" punchlines that hit one after the other. With more than four decades of experience in comedy clubs, theatres, television, and radio, Beaz has built a career around turning everyday situations into big laughs. His shows combine a storyteller's eye for the unexpected with a well-honed sense of timing, creating a warm, laughter-filled experience audiences can share with friends and family. Known to viewers of Discovery Channel's Moonshiners, Beaz is celebrating eleven seasons on the hit series while continuing to perform regularly at the Grand Ole Opry and touring nationwide. The award-winning entertainer has been signed with both Sony and Warner Brothers Records, featured on Dry Bar Comedy (Southern by the Grace of God), appeared on hundreds of television broadcasts, and co-hosts the television series Christian Comedy Revival. Beaz's reach extends across syndicated radio programs including The Bob & Tom Show, Big D & Bubba, The John Boy & Billy Big Show, The Phil Valentine Show, and Rick & Bubba, as well as SiriusXM comedy channels. His work has drawn praise from Rolling Stone, which declared, "Beaz is 'Killer!'", while Country Weekly described his material as "Gut-busting takes on life!" "Having been given the opportunity to make people laugh and smile for all these years is truly a blessing!" says Beaz. "And being able to partner with American Legion Post #250 to support their Toys For Tots Drive makes this show even more special." Whether audiences know him from television, radio, his comedy recordings, or the Opry stage, the "Operation: Holly Jolly Christmas" Tour puts his live stand-up front and center for a memorable night of holiday humor that helps bring Christmas cheer to local children in need. Killer Beaz brings "Operation: Holly Jolly Christmas" to American Legion Post #250 Fowl River in Theodore, Alabama, on Saturday, December 12. Show begins at 6:30 PM. The performance is open to the public, and community members are encouraged to attend and support the cause. For tickets, tour dates, venue details, and ticket links, visitKillerBeaz.com. ABOUT KILLER BEAZ Killer Beaz is a beloved national touring comedian, television personality, and recording artist whose career spans more than four decades. Known to millions of radio listeners and television viewers alike, Beaz has spent eleven seasons on Discovery Channel's Moonshiners, performs regularly at the Grand Ole Opry, and co-hosts Christian Comedy Revival. Doors Open 5:30 for VIP/Meet and Greet, meet and greet and will end promptly at 6:15 Doors open for general seating at 6:00 NO REFUNDS
