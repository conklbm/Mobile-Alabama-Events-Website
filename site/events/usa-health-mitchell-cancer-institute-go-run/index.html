About

Join us for the 19th annual GO Run on Saturday, Sept. 26, 2026, at Hancock Whitney Stadium. The 5K Race will start at 8 a.m. and the 1-Mile Fun Run will start at 9 a.m. This fundraising event, presented by the Catranis Family Charitable Foundation, supports gynecologic cancer research at USA Health Mitchell Cancer Institute. Over the past 19 years, GO Run has raised more than $1 million in support of MCI. Whether you run or walk, online registration is now open! Register today at usahealthsystem.com/gorun Interested in being a GO Run sponsor? Please contact Shanna Thorpe (USA Health Development) at sthorpe@southalabama.edu
