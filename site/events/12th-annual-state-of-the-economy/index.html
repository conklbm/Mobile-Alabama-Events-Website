    12th Annual State of the Economy

    Wed Nov 18 · 7:30 am – 10 am

    6 S. Joachim St. Mobile AL 36602-2708

    CommunityArts & Theater

    About

    IMPORTANT INFORMATION: Due to increased security measures imposed by the Saenger Theatre, the doors will be opening at 7:15 AM.Please note there is a CLEAR BAG POLICY: one (1) clear bag no larger than 12"x12"x8" is allowed per person. Approved medically necessary devices are allowed.Metal detectors will also be in operation. EVENT DETAILS:Join the Mobile Chamber for the 12th Annual State of the Economy, happening Wednesday, November 18, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. at the historic Saenger Theatre in downtown Mobile. Be among the first to see the results of the 2027 Mobile Area Economic Outlook Survey, offering exclusive data and projections to help guide your business decisions. We'll also recognize innovation and leadership by announcing the Manufacturer of the Year and Innovator of the Year awards spotlighting companies driving growth and progress in our community. Don't miss this opportunity to gain valuable knowledge, make key connections, and celebrate the strength of Mobile's economy.
