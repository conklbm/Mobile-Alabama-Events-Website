About

Join us for “Art of the Paw” on September 19, 2026! Eastern Shore Art Center invites the community to engage in creativity and education as we celebrate our furry friends! Guests will enjoy interactive booths, a pop-up art exhibit, art projects, and more. Open to ALL AGES. Free admission! Event Details: Adoption event with Baldwin Humane Society Educational Vendors with Hollywood Feed and Thompson Animal Hospital Make-and-Take art projects Live Pet Portraits – Bring a photograph of your pet!
