About

The Lower Alabama AI (LA-AI) Meetup is a community-focused group based in Fairhope and Mobile, Alabama, designed for individuals interested in the field of artificial intelligence (AI). It serves as a forum for people of all skill levels, from AI novices to seasoned experts, to come together and discuss the latest trends, technologies, and applications in artificial intelligence. Meetings and Locations The LA-AI Meetup gathers bi-monthly in Fairhope at the PNC Bank Building (36 N Section St, Fairhope, AL 36532) on the 1st and 3rd Fridays at 3 PM. An additional monthly meeting occurs in Mobile at the Innovation Portal on the last Friday of every month. These sessions are designed to be interactive and informative, offering attendees a chance to learn through demos of current AI applications, engage in discussions about AI use cases and the latest developments, and network with local AI practitioners, business leaders, and technology enthusiasts. Mission and Activities The core mission of the LA-AI Meetup is to foster a community that promotes learning and collaboration in the AI space. By bringing together diverse individuals and perspectives, the group aims to propel local businesses and the wider community forward into the future of technology. The activities at each meeting are structured to facilitate knowledge sharing, emphasizing real-world applications of AI and how these innovations can impact various industries and everyday life. Community and Participation The LA-AI Meetup encourages active participation from all attendees, creating an environment where questions are welcomed and knowledge exchange is paramount. It aims to build a supportive network where individuals can learn about AI, share their experiences, and collaborate on projects or ideas. Whether you want to deepen your understanding of AI, explore how AI can be applied in your field, or meet like-minded individuals with a passion for technology, the LA-AI Meetup offers a valuable platform to connect and grow.
