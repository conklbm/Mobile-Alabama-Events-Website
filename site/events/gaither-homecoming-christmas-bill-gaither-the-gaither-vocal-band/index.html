About

Celebrate the wonder of the season with the 2026 Gaither Homecoming Christmas tour. Join Bill Gaither, the beloved Gaither Vocal Band, and an incredible lineup of special guests for an unforgettable evening of Christmas music, Gospel favorites, laughter, and worship. Filled with joyful harmonies, cherished holiday classics, and inspiring songs of faith, Gaither Homecoming Christmas celebrates the birth of Christ and the hope, peace, and love that define the season. From timeless Christmas carols to beloved Gospel favorites, each performance offers moments of celebration, reflection, and encouragement that have made Gaither Homecoming events a treasured holiday tradition for generations. Sharing the stage this Christmas will be special guests Mark Lowry, The Hoppers, Chris Blue, and many more. Whether you're continuing a family tradition or attending your very first Homecoming event, Gaither Homecoming Christmas is an evening designed to bring people together through the joy of music, the warmth of fellowship, and the timeless message of Christmas.
