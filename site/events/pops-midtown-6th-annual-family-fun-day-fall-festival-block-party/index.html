    About

    POP’S MIDTOWN 6TH ANNUAL FAMILY FUN DAY FALL FESTIVAL & BLOCK PARTY Mobile, get ready – our biggest fall celebration of the year is back! Join us Saturday, October 17, from 10 AM–6 PM for a full day of family fun in the Heart of Mobile. Admission is FREE, there are no lines to enter, and this year’s festival is bigger and better than ever! Here’s what’s waiting for you: Exotic Petting Zoo Giant Inflatable Park Face Painting & Kids’ Carnival Games ALL-NEW Corn Room – yes, an entire room! Brand-new viral-themed photo spots Live Music & Entertainment Special Character Appearances Frozen Apple Cider & Fall Treats Food Trucks & Local Vendor Market The Best Little Pumpkin Patch in Alabama …and plenty more surprises! NEW THIS YEAR: We’re trading individual attraction tickets for easy-to-use wristbands! Wristbands will be available for purchase for carnival games and participating attractions. Come explore our pumpkin patch, find your favorite fall photo spot, enjoy the entertainment, and make a full day of it with the people you love. Pop’s Midtown Pumpkin Patch – your fall family tradition 7 years strong! Saturday, October 17 10 AM–6 PM FREE admission | Attraction wristbands available for purchase Pop’s Midtown Garden Center 2103 Airport Blvd. Midtown Mobile, AL 36606
