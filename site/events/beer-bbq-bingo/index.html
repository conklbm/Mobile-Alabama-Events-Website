Listed by 92ZEW.

Free bingo with the 92ZEW crew at Moe's. Tuesdays downtown, Thursdays in Daphne. Show up early for a table.

Parking: Springhill Ave lot fills by 6:45; street parking on Warren St.

Kids: Works for kids under 6.

About

Join 92ZEW live at Moe’s Original BBQ for free Bingo each week! Win great prizes, enjoy award winning BBQ and a cold one with Matt & Gene Tuesdays at Moe’s Original BBQ in Downtown Mobile and on Thursdays, Clint & Cornbred bring the fun to Moe’s in Daphne! Both nights start at 7PM.
