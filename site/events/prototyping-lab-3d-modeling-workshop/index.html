About

Beginner-to-intermediate 3D modeling workshop Think CAD is complicated? Not here. We’ll teach you the basics, give you the tools, and let you create—all in one evening. Learn 3D modeling from scratch with a 15-minute lecture, a tour of our prototype lab, and a full hands-on session using OnShape. This workshop is open to STEAM educators, students, and creatives of all levels. Bring your laptop, charger, and mouse. Sign up here: https://links.innovation-portal.com/WDRTqge
