About

Mobile Bay Events is a weekly-updated calendar for Mobile, the Eastern Shore, and Dauphin Island. It's built and run by Brooks Conkle, who lives here and got tired of checking fifteen websites to find out what was happening on Saturday.

How it works

Every Thursday a script pulls venue calendars, the 92ZEW and Mob Mom community calendars, city calendars, and Ticketmaster, then merges duplicates so each event shows once. Anything the software isn't sure about waits for a human. Every listing links to the original source; we don't sell tickets.

Where the listings come from

  • 92ZEW
  • City of Gulf Shores
  • City of Orange Beach
  • Innovation Portal
  • Joe Jefferson Players
  • Mobile SPCA
  • Mobile Symphony Orchestra
  • The Mob Mom
  • Ticketmaster
  • A weekly hand check of local Facebook pages

Ticketmaster listings are provided via the Ticketmaster Discovery API. Event images belong to the venue or organizer credited under each image.

Missing something?

Venues and organizers: if your calendar is public and machine-readable, we can add it in a week. Get in touch.
